# Privacy

The MVP analyzes email content locally in the browser.

- Email bodies, sender addresses, subjects, URLs, and attachment names are not transmitted.
- Analysis results are session-scoped unless a future setting explicitly changes that behavior.
- The extension does not read the entire inbox or unrelated pages.
- The extension does not download attachments, open URLs, submit forms, or enter credentials.
- Any future external service must be opt-in and documented before implementation.
